Remember this

By the 1960s legal segregation was falling in the South, but in the northern cities poverty, poor housing and police brutality carried on. Black Power grew in that gap, where the civil rights wins had not reached.

That gap is where your essay starts: the conditions the movement was answering. It is one of the points you will use when you build an essay.
