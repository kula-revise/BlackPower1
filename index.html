The essay so far

One move down. This is the first sentence of the argument you will hand in at the end of Lesson 5.

Black Power grew out of conditions that the Civil Rights Movement's legal victories never reached, so its militancy began as an answer to continued poverty and police brutality.

Notice what that sentence does not do. It does not say the Civil Rights Movement failed, and it does not defend everything Black Power went on to do. It explains a turn. That is what a marker means by a line of argument.
